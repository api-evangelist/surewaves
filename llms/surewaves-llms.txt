# SureWaves

> SureWaves (SureWaves MediaTech) is a broadcast-television advertising technology company that integrates linear TV with digital advertising infrastructure. It sells AI-powered spot and yield management to broadcasters — automating electronic order entry, traffic, make-goods, ADUs, log times, postings, audit trail and billing — and real-time, pay-for-performance campaign tooling to advertisers. It also operates India's largest spot TV network, more than 20 million spots a month across 550+ local channels reaching 490+ million people. Founded 2006 in Bangalore by Rajendra Khare, Anant Kansal and Tapan Datta; 50+ patents; backed by Accel, Canaan and India Innovation Fund.
